2 The Service

LODE provides a travel data analytics platform that allows the Subscriber to upload, query, and analyse travel and expense data. The Service operates in two modes:

Local Lakes

Subscriber Data is stored and processed entirely within the Subscriber's browser. Nothing is uploaded to LODE servers.

Cloud Lakes

Subscriber Data is stored in encrypted cloud storage and accessible to Authorised Users within the Subscriber's organisation.
